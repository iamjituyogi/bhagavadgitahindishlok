Privacy Policy

Bhagwat Geeta Hindi Audio (Android app) · Effective date: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bhagwat Geeta Hindi Audio (“the app”, “we”, “our”), developed by Jitendra Yogi, handles information when you use the app. We built the app to let you listen to the Bhagavad Gita in Hindi, and we keep data collection to the minimum needed for that.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember your choices, the app saves the following only on your device: your theme (light/dark), playback preferences (such as speed), favourite chapters, listening history, playback position and the last chapter played. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

3. Network use and third-party services

  • Audio streaming: chapter audio is streamed over the internet from our audio host (mp3.smartjpr.com). Like any web request, the host can see your IP address and basic request details in its server logs.
  • Firebase Realtime Database (Google): the app reads the latest available app version so it can tell you when an update is available. This is a read-only check and does not send personal information. Google may process technical data such as your IP address and device/app identifiers to provide this service, as described in the Firebase privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

  • Internet: to stream audio and check for updates.
  • Notifications and foreground service (media playback): to show playback controls and keep audio playing when the screen is off or the app is in the background.

5. Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Data security

Because personal data is not collected or sent to our servers, there is no account or personal database for us to protect. Network requests use secure connections where the service supports them.

7. Your choices

You can delete all locally stored app data at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. You can also revoke the notification permission in your device settings.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
